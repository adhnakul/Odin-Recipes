World's Best Lasagna

Ingrediants

  • Meat sauce: 1 lb (450g) ground beef/sausage, 1 chopped onion, 2 minced garlic cloves, 24 oz (700ml) marinara sauce, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning.
  • Cheese filling: 15 oz (425g) ricotta (or cottage cheese), 1 egg, ½ cup grated Parmesan, salt, pepper.
  • Assembly: 9–12 oven-ready lasagna sheets, 2½ cups shredded mozzarella.

Steps

  1. Prep Heat: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Make Sauce: Brown the ground meat with onion and garlic in a skillet. Drain excess fat, stir in marinara sauce and Italian seasoning, then simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Mix Cheese: In a bowl, mix ricotta, egg, Parmesan, salt, and pepper until combined.
  4. Layer (9x13 inch pan):
    • Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom.
    • Add a layer of lasagna sheets.
    • Spread half the ricotta mixture, then sprinkle 3/4 cup mozzarella.
    • Add a layer of meat sauce.
    • Repeat layers (noodles, ricotta, mozzarella, sauce).
    • Top with final layer of noodles, remaining meat sauce, and top with remaining mozzarella.
  5. Bake: Cover with foil (tented so it doesn't stick to cheese) and bake for 25 minutes. Uncover and bake for another 15 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  6. Rest: Let rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing.
